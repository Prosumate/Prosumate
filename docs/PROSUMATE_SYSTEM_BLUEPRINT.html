3. Technical Deep-Dive by Domain

Part I: Architecture & Multi-Tenancy Live
  • agencyId (Company/Parent) isolates platform SaaS billing and agency users.
  • locationId (Workspace/Sub-Account) isolates operational CRM, messaging, funnels, and workflows.
  • Zero cross-tenant bleed: Verified via automated test suite rejecting cross-tenant resource operations with HTTP 403.
  • Dual-seam repository architecture allowing in-process MemoryDatabase or PostgreSQL Drizzle persistence without touching controllers.
Part II: CRM & Opportunity Pipeline Live
  • Contacts & Companies: Master identity records with dynamic tags, custom fields, notes, tasks, and DND status.
  • Opportunities & Pipelines: Visual Kanban stages, monetary deal values, contact linkage, and stage-transition event emitters.
  • Smart search, multi-field filtering, and deterministic deduplication on email and phone numbers.
Part III: Unified Communications 100% Native
  • Internal Email: Local mailbox storage, HTML sanitization, open & click tracking, and deliverability scoring (>80%).
  • Internal Telephony/SMS: Virtual phone pool, GSM-7 / UCS-2 character segmentation, and STOP/START carrier compliance.
  • External Adapters: Zero-downtime fallback to Twilio for SMS and Resend for Email when credentials are configured.
Part IV: Workflow Automation Engine Live
  • Visual Node Canvas: Flowchart canvas with trigger nodes, conditional branches, action steps, and time delays.
  • Triggers: CONTACT_CREATED, FORM_SUBMITTED, OPPORTUNITY_STAGE_CHANGED, APPOINTMENT_BOOKED.
  • Actions: Email, SMS, Add/Remove Tags, Update Fields, Wait Delays, Webhooks, AI Actions.
  • Execution Audit: Step-by-step audit records tracking input payloads, status, and completion times.
Part VI: Websites, Funnels & Lead Capture Live
  • Visual WYSIWYG Builder: Sections, multi-column containers, headings, buttons, forms, and image studios.
  • Template Library: Pre-built 16:9 agency templates with live preview and one-click clone.

Part VII: 3-Plane Commerce & Billing 100% Native
  • Plane 1: Agency SaaS subscription billing (Starter, Growth, Pro).
  • Plane 2: Location credit wallet with automated threshold top-up and rebilling markup (e.g. 20% margin).
  • Plane 3: Customer invoices with Luhn card verification, coupon codes (PROSU20), tax rates, and HTML/PDF generation.
Part X: Analytics, Governance & RBAC Live
  • Multi-Touch Attribution: Tracks first-touch and last-touch channels, calculating ROAS and CAC automatically.
  • Sales Leaderboard: Won opportunities aggregated by staff rep.
  • Append-Only Audit Log: Every mutation records actor, IP, timestamp, resource ID, and delta payload.
  • Granular RBAC: Strict permissions matrix for Platform Admins, Agency Owners, and Location Staff.

4. Architectural Advantages over Standard GHL

Prosumate was intentionally engineered with architectural advancements that solve common high-cost friction points found in typical GoHighLevel deployments.
Advantage 1: 100% Native Zero-Dependency Architecture ($0 Initial Run Cost) Key Differentiator

Standard GoHighLevel instances are completely paralyzed without live third-party accounts (Twilio for phone, Mailgun/SendGrid for email, Stripe for payments, OpenAI for intelligence, and external n8n/webhook servers for automation).

Prosumate's Breakthrough: Every core service includes a high-fidelity Internal Native Provider running locally with zero external API fees. Developers, quality assurance teams, and enterprise testing suites run completely offline at $0 cost, while production can toggle live external carrier transports on-demand.

Advantage 2: Unified In-Process Event Bus & Queue High Performance

Prosumate replaces the mandatory requirement of a separate Redis cluster with a high-throughput, in-process cache, pub/sub event bus, and retry queue. This dramatically simplifies deployment on single-node VPS, lightweight containers, or serverless environments.
